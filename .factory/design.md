# Diagram Source Studio — visual thesis

## Direction

**Night-market neon signage.** Diagram source is treated like a sign-maker's wiring plan: the editor is the dark workbench, rendered nodes are luminous tubes, and renderer differences are inspection chalk. This fits a verification tool because bright marks expose small faults without turning the interface into a generic dark IDE.

The site and app use one explicit dark treatment. It is a focused desktop instrument, not a themeable content site.

## Tokens

- `--ink-950 #080a0d`: page and canvas; the street after closing.
- `--ink-900 #10141a`: workbench surfaces.
- `--ink-800 #19202a`: raised controls and dividers.
- `--paper #f5f1e8`: primary text, like pale sign-maker paper; 17.2:1 on ink.
- `--muted #b9c0c8`: secondary text; 9.5:1 on ink.
- `--cyan #36f1e4`: primary action and focus; 13.2:1 on ink.
- `--amber #ffc857`: diagnostic warning and paid marker; 12.1:1 on ink.
- `--magenta #ff4fa3`: renderer-difference marks; 6.2:1 on ink.
- `--green #74e39a`: verified/success; 11.3:1 on ink.
- `--red #ff7070`: errors paired with text/icons; 7.0:1 on ink.

Spacing follows 4/8 px: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. Content measures stay below 70 characters. Corners are clipped at one corner, like a metal sign backing plate. Rules are thin and bright only when state needs attention.

## Type

- Display: `Barlow Condensed`, self-hosted WOFF2, 600/700. Tall storefront proportions suit compact engineering headlines.
- Body and utility: `Atkinson Hyperlegible`, self-hosted WOFF2, 400/700. Open counters keep diagnostics readable.
- Source: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace`; no extra font payload.

If bundled font files fail, local system fallbacks preserve the hierarchy. Body text is at least 16px and mobile text at least 17px.

## Layout and shape language

The landing first screen is left-weighted like a row of shop bays: copy occupies the narrow lit lane and the original renderer illustration fills the wider bay. It is not a centered hero. Section labels use small numbered tabs. In the app, a slim rail holds project and diagnostics controls; source and preview share the remaining bench. On phones, source and preview become switchable full-width panes and nonessential metadata drops below the fold.

Buttons are solid cyan plates for the primary action and dark outlined plates for secondary actions. Cards appear only for independent renderer results. A recurring three-dot “tube test” motif distinguishes the product at thumbnail size.

## Interaction and motion

The signature motion is **tube warm-up**: on first reveal, cyan paths move from low opacity to steady light over 240ms while comparison marks arrive from their source edge. App state changes use 160–240ms opacity and transform transitions. Nothing loops. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, paths and panels appear immediately and scrolling is not animated.

Keyboard focus uses a 3px cyan outline plus a 2px ink offset. All targets are at least 44px. Preview changes are announced through a polite live region. Pane tabs use arrow-key navigation.
